# Solution

- `replace(beg, end, old_val, new_val);`
	- Replace all occurrences of `old_val` between `beg` and `end` with `new_val`.
- `replace_if(beg, end, pred, new_val);`
	- Replace all occurrences of `old_val` between `beg` and `end` with `new_val` if they return `true` at predicate.
- `replace_copy(beg, end, dest, old_val, new_val);`
	- Replace all occurrences of `old_val` between `beg` and `end` with `new_val` but put in `dest`.
- `replace_copy_if(beg, end, dest, pred, new_val);`
	- Replace all occurrences of `old_val` between `beg` and `end` with `new_val` but put in `dest`, if they return `true` at predicate.
